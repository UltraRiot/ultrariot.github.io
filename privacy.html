Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Doppel Drop (Android package com.fjordlightstudio.doppeldrop) handles information. The game is provided by Robert Dalsegg Kjersem, an independent developer using the name Fjordlight Studio. Robert Dalsegg Kjersem is the data controller for the processing described as carried out by the developer in this policy. Fjordlight Studio is a developer name, not a separate registered company. For privacy questions or requests, contact rkjersem@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

Doppel Drop saves settings and gameplay progress, including personal high scores, daily best scores and selected cosmetic trails, locally on your device. The current game does not require you to create a game account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes local game data, subject to any backup and restore features managed by your device or operating system.

Advertising and third-party services

The game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It uses banner advertisements on menu, pause and result screens and interstitial advertisements between eligible rounds.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address (which can be used to estimate a general location), device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses these data for purposes including serving and measuring advertisements, analytics, security and fraud prevention. The specific processing depends on your location, choices, device settings and the advertising services in use.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. Google's SDK disclosure is available in the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure documentation.

Your privacy choices

Where required, a Google User Messaging Platform message allows you to make advertising privacy choices. When available, the game's settings contain an Ad Privacy option for reviewing those choices. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID and adjust advertising privacy controls in your Android settings. The advertising available after a choice may differ by region and by Google's supported ad modes.

Where applicable law requires consent for advertising-related processing, that processing depends on the choices collected through the consent message. We handle support messages to respond to your request and pursue our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the game. We may retain or disclose information where necessary to meet legal obligations. Google's own legal bases and controller responsibilities are explained in its policies.

Information you send us

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to answer you, investigate the issue and maintain relevant support correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

Sharing, storage and security

We do not operate a separate game account or high-score server in the current version. Local progress stays in the app's device storage unless handled by operating-system backup features. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising service providers under their policies and may be processed outside your country. Google's SDK documentation states that SDK data transmitted to Google are encrypted in transit using TLS.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond to the request, handle follow-up questions or meet applicable obligations. Google's retention practices are described in its privacy policy. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your requests

You can remove local game data through Android's app-storage controls. You may email rkjersem@gmail.com to ask about personal information in support correspondence or to request its deletion, subject to applicable obligations. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls and request channels. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent or complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The current advertising configuration is not intended for a child-directed release. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest update. Material changes will be communicated as required.
